# Overlay ideas for the editor (a list only; nothing gets built until his footage is in)

Pieces the editor could pull from this project once he has filmed. Each one is either a frame
or clip that already exists or a quick render from the code.

1. **Scan sweep**: the heat map filling in across the island, "860 landing spots scored"
   (`2026-11-24-fortnite-chest-route-scan-clip.mp4`, 0-2.4 s).
2. **Top 5 board**: the ranked list with numbered pins (`…-top5-still.png`). Fits while he says "I scored every landing spot".
3. **Route drawing itself** with the chest counter and timer (scan clip 4.4-10.9 s, or `…-clip.mp4`).
4. **Average drop 24 vs this route 31**: the end card of the scan clip. That's the payoff number.
5. **Route card** (`…-card.png`): to hold up beside his gameplay, or to show for a second as "here's the route".
6. **Split check**: his real chest-by-chest time next to the predicted time (needs his run; `route.scan --calibrate`).
7. **NP-hard beat**: "every chest you add doubles the work": the exact solver takes 0.35 s at 16 chests, 1.4 s at 18, and weeks at 40.
8. **His class code on screen**: `class/main.py` Dijkstra, with the line "this is the part I wrote for class".
9. **Token reality line**: 1 in 137,500 per chest (Epic), so even this route is ~1 in 6,600 games. Only as a
   wink at the end; don't promise a token.
10. **Bus-path variant**: the first clip (`…-clip.mp4`) shows a bus line and the drop. Useful if he wants a
    "depends on the bus" beat.
